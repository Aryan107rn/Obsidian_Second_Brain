## How this vault is organized
- **Flat topic notes, dedicated algorithm sub-notes.** Major DSA topics (Arrays, Strings, etc.) are single deep notes covering all their patterns; individual named algorithms too specific for a topic note (or spanning multiple topics) live in `DSA/Algorithms/`.
- **Every folder has an MOC.** Use it as the entry point — it links to everything else in that folder and shows what's covered vs. still queued.
- **Notes are updated only on request**, not automatically after every conversation — this MOC and the sub-MOCs reflect the vault's actual current state.
